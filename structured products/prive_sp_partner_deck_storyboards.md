# Privé Structured Products Engine — Partner Deck Storyboards

**Prepared:** 6 August 2026 (rev. 2 — evidence from *SP_research.pdf* deep-dive folded in) · **Scope:** Slide-by-slide storyboards for two partner-facing decks. Not final designs.
**Conventions:** UK English. Headlines are full sentences carrying the argument (assertion–evidence). One idea per slide. All on-slide statistics carry a small-text source line. ⚠ marks a claim needing a proof point Privé may not yet hold — see the Proof-Point Gap Register at the end.

## DECK A — Referral Partner Version ("The Forwardable Pitch")

**Design brief in one line:** built for the second reader — a Head of Investment Products or SP desk head reading cold on a phone, no narrator, ~90 seconds of attention. Every slide passes the "headline-only" test. The referrer's reputation rides on it: nothing salesy, nothing he'd have to apologise for.

**Format notes:** 9 slides. 16:9 but designed to survive phone-portrait skim: one headline, one visual, max ~40 words of body per slide. Because there is no speaker, the "speaker intent" column below is written as **reader intent** — what the slide must do to a cold reader.

## DECK B — Consulting Partner Version (Deloitte / Synpulse archetypes)

The "where we need you" section is the differentiator: it hands the partner revenue, not just a referral fee.

**Structure decision:** one deck of 12 slides with **flagged variant slides** rather than two decks — slides B2, B5 and B12 carry lens variants; everything else is common.
