### Module 4: The Incumbent Vendor Strategy — Temenos, Avaloq, Iress

The wealth management technology sector is currently undergoing a structural metamorphosis, transitioning from the "Digital Convenience" era (Wealth 2.0) to a state of "Intelligent Advisory" (Wealth 3.0). This shift is driven by the strategic pivot of incumbent vendors—Temenos, Avaloq, and Iress—who are aggressively embedding Generative AI (GenAI) and Agentic AI into their core platforms to maintain dominance against nimbler fintech challengers. By 2027, the "standard" wealth management platform will no longer be a passive system of record but an active orchestration layer capable of autonomous decision-making, hyper-personalized portfolio construction, and real-time regulatory compliance.

#### I. Temenos: The Agentic Orchestrator
Temenos has positioned its 2027 strategy under the theme of "Trust, Modernize, Transcend," emphasizing a move from AI experimentation to agentic impact. The cornerstone of this strategy is the **June 8, 2026, acquisition of additiv**, a Swiss wealth tech specialist, which provided Temenos with a mature "wealth orchestration" layer.

**1. Product Roadmap and the "Additiv" Integration**
The integration of additiv’s *addWealth* platform into the Temenos core has fundamentally altered the vendor's value proposition. By 2027, Temenos offers a "Composable Wealth" architecture that allows financial institutions to reduce the time-to-market for new investment products from the historical 12-month average to just **3–6 months** (Source: [Temenos Investor Relations, June 2026]). This is achieved through a library of pre-configured AI modules that can be "plugged" into the core banking system via a unified orchestration layer.

**2. Key AI Modules: Copilots and Agents**
Temenos has moved beyond simple chatbots to **Temenos Copilot for Wealth**, a conversational assistant integrated directly into the advisor desktop.
*   **Agentic AI for Wealth:** By 2027, Temenos has transitioned from "Copilots" (which assist humans) to "Agents" (which execute tasks). These autonomous agents are designed for high-friction workflows such as **Financial Crime Mitigation (FCM)** and **Automated Client Onboarding**.
*   **Explainable AI (XAI):** A critical differentiator for Temenos is its patented XAI framework. Unlike "black box" AI, every recommendation made by the Temenos AI is backed by an audit trail that explains the logic behind the decision, ensuring compliance with global transparency regulations like the EU's AI Act (Source: [Gartner Market Guide 2024]).

**3. Pricing and Commercial Tiers**
Temenos has successfully transitioned its base to a **SaaS-first model**, reporting an Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) of over **$1.2 billion by late 2026**.
*   **Add-on Pricing:** AI features like the "Product Manager Copilot" are sold as premium add-ons. Pricing is typically **volume-based**, tied to either the number of active accounts or Assets Under Management (AUM).
*   **Bespoke Tiers:** While entry-level AI tools are bundled in top-tier SaaS subscriptions, advanced agentic capabilities require custom pricing agreements based on the bank's transaction volume and complexity (Source: [Windsor Drake Analyst Report 2026]).

#### II. Avaloq: The Hyper-Personalization Specialist
Avaloq, under the ownership of the **NEC Group**, has leveraged its deep private banking roots to lead the market in **Hyper-Personalization**. Their 2027 roadmap focuses on bridging the "Advisory Gap" by providing HNW-level service to the mass-affluent segment.

**1. The Hyper-Personalization Engine**
Avaloq’s flagship AI innovation is its **Hyper-Personalization Engine**, which can generate **millions of individualized portfolios** simultaneously. This moves away from the traditional "model portfolio" approach, where clients were grouped into a handful of risk buckets.
*   **ESG Integration:** The engine is "data provider agnostic," allowing banks to integrate ESG feeds from Morningstar, MSCI, or Refinitiv. It uses real-time optimization to maximize sustainability impact while maintaining the client's risk-return profile (Source: [Avaloq Technical Documentation, 2025]).
*   **BlackRock Partnership:** The integration of **BlackRock’s Aladdin Wealth** risk analytics into the Avaloq platform (fully live by March 2026 for early adopters like CMB Monaco) provides the engine with institutional-grade risk modeling capabilities.

**2. Technical Architecture: Cloud-Native and API-First**
Avaloq has spent 2024–2026 containerizing its services into a microservices-based architecture.
*   **Data Streaming:** The platform uses **Apache Kafka** for real-time data streaming, enabling "near real-time decisioning" where portfolios rebalance instantly based on market shocks or life events.
*   **Connectivity:** With over **7,500 REST API endpoints**, Avaloq provides the most open ecosystem among the big three, allowing firms to "plug and play" third-party AI fintechs via the *Avaloq.one* marketplace (Source: [Avaloq Developer Portal]).

**3. AI Modules: Avaloq Engage**
Launched as a virtual assistant for relationship managers (RMs), **Avaloq Engage** has evolved by 2027 into a full AI engagement suite. It uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to read client messages on platforms like WhatsApp or WeChat and propose "smart actions" directly to the RM, reducing administrative response times by **up to 40%**.

#### III. Iress: The Practical ROI Transformation
Iress has undergone a "Big Shift" transformation, selling off non-core assets to focus exclusively on Wealth and Trading. Their strategy is defined by **"Practical AI"**—focusing on immediate advisor productivity rather than speculative long-term R&D.

**1. The Thoughtworks Partnership**
In May 2026, Iress announced a landmark partnership with **Thoughtworks** to modernize **Xplan**. Using Thoughtworks' **AI/works™ platform**, Iress has reverse-engineered its legacy logic into modular, AI-ready components. This "staged modernization" avoids the risks of a full system replacement while enabling rapid feature deployment (Source: [Iress Press Release, May 2026]).

**2. Statements of Advice (SoA) Automation**
The most impactful 2027 feature for Iress is the full automation of the **Statement of Advice (SoA)**. Historically a 5-7 hour manual task, Iress’s AI agents (integrated via partners like **FAYBL** and **Zatersio**) now produce **80%-complete SoAs in under 90 minutes**.
*   **Workflow Orchestration:** Iress uses "Agentic AI" to handle end-to-end workflows: from meeting transcription (via **PlannerPal**) to data entry, strategy generation, and final compliance checks.

**3. Standardized Pricing and Points-Based Tiers**
Iress has eliminated its legacy "sweetheart" deals in favor of a **standardized rate card**.
*   **AI Usage Points:** To manage the high compute costs of GenAI, Iress uses a **points-based system**. Firms purchase "AI Credits" that are consumed based on the complexity of the task (e.g., a full SoA consumes more points than a simple meeting summary).
*   **Subscription Base:** Standard Xplan seats remain on a monthly licensee fee, with AI credits acting as a flexible top-up (Source: [Financial Newswire AU, June 2026]).

#### IV. 2027 Standard AI Feature Checklist
By 2027, the following features are considered "table stakes" for any top-tier wealth management platform:

1.  **Autonomous Life-Event Planning:** AI that detects life changes via transaction data and triggers proactive advice.
2.  **Explainable AI (XAI) Audit Trails:** Mandatory transparency on why an AI recommendation was made.
3.  **Real-Time Suitability Monitoring:** Automated blocking of trades that violate client risk profiles or ESG constraints.
4.  **Behavioral Sentiment Analysis:** Real-time monitoring of client anxiety levels during market volatility.
5.  **Meeting "Debrief" Automation:** Instant CRM updates and follow-up drafts after advisor-client interactions.
