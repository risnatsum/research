# The Intelligent Alpha: Privé’s Strategic AI Roadmap

**Purpose:** Secure Board approval for the three-pillar AI strategy and the "Avid/Alcos/Demonos" product vision to transition Privé into the Wealth 3.0 era.
**Audience:** Executive / C-suite
**Date:** May 22, 2024

## Audience profile

- **Mindset:** Pragmatic and ROI-driven; they view AI as an industrial tool to solve margin compression and scale AUM without proportional headcount growth.
- **Care about:** Measurable advisor productivity (15+ hours recovered), the 7.2x "Leader Multiplier" ROI, and maintaining a "Regulatory Shield" against tightening HKMA/MAS standards.
- **Skeptical of:** AI "hallucinations" in advisory, high R&D costs without near-term wins, and the "black box" risk of unexplainable models.
- **Success signal:** Approval of the 2024–2027 roadmap and the shift to outcome-based monetization models.

## Narrative arc

Situation → Complication → Resolution

The presentation begins by framing the global shift to Wealth 3.0—moving from simple digital access to "Intelligent Advisory." It then identifies the critical complication: the "70/30" administrative drag that is eroding advisor margins. The resolution is presented through Privé’s three-pillar strategy, culminating in a vision of Autonomous Fiduciary Agents that decouple AUM growth from operational costs.
