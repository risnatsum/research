## 1. Executive Summary: The Structural Tipping Point

The European wealth management industry has entered a period of terminal structural consolidation. This transition is not merely a cyclical trend but a permanent realignment driven by a "perfect storm" of demographic exhaustion, regulatory overburden, and the arrival of aggressive private equity (PE) capital. At the center of this storm is the "Succession Gap"—a demographic time bomb where a generation of firm founders, predominantly "Baby Boomers," are reaching retirement age without viable internal successors.
