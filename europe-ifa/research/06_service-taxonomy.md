# Module 6: Service Taxonomy & Revenue Model Mapping

## 1. Executive Summary: The Death of the Transaction
By 2026, the European IFA market has reached a tipping point in its revenue evolution. The traditional "commission-on-sale" model, once the lifeblood of the industry, is being replaced by a "Wealth Engineering" model centered on recurring AUM fees and fixed service retainers. This shift is driven by the **MiFID II/MiFIR Review** and the **Retail Investment Strategy (RIS)**, which have introduced "Value for Money" benchmarks that are difficult to satisfy under opaque commission structures.

## 2. Revenue Model Distribution: The Three-Speed Europe
| Maturity Level | Primary Model | Countries | Revenue Mix (Fee vs Comm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High** | Fee-Only / AUM | UK, Netherlands | 95% Fee / 5% Comm |
| **Medium** | Hybrid / Pivot | France, Spain, Italy | 65% Fee / 35% Comm |
| **Low** | Commission-Heavy | Germany, Portugal | 20% Fee / 80% Comm |

### 2.1 The "Scissors Effect"
Total AUM across European retail markets has surged to **€33 trillion**, yet revenue margins are compressing. The average ongoing advice fee has stabilized at **77 bps** in the UK, while in France and Germany, "retrocessions" (kickbacks from fund managers) are being replaced by explicit management fees.

## 3. Service Taxonomy: Beyond Investment Advice
To justify fees in a low-yield, high-transparency environment, IFAs are expanding their service stack.
- **Pensions & Retirement (45% of Rev):** Remains the "anchor" service, particularly with SIPP (UK), Riester (DE), and Assurance-Vie (FR).
- **Tax & Estate Planning (15% of Rev):** The fastest-growing segment, driven by wealth tax changes in Spain and inheritance tax freezes in the UK and France.
- **ESG/Sustainable Advice (10% of Rev):** Now offered by 62% of firms, but actual AUM impact is limited by technical complexity.
- **Mortgage & Protection (10% of Rev):** Vital for "Full Life-Cycle" advice, particularly in the UK and Netherlands.

## 4. The "Fiscal Friction" of Cross-Border Advice
A high-margin niche (370+ specialized firms) has emerged to serve the 10.5 million EU expats. These firms utilize "Spanish Compliant Bonds" or "Luxembourg Life Wrappers" to navigate the complex tax interplay between jurisdictions.

## 5. Strategic Conclusion
The winners in 2026 are those who have successfully "de-coupled" their revenue from investment performance. Firms charging for "Financial Planning" as a distinct, billable service (rather than a free add-on to asset management) report 15% higher profit margins.
